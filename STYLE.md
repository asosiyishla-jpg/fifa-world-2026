## 1. WRITING PATTERNS & GOLD STANDARDS

### Pattern 1 — The Analytical Hook (Opening the breakdown)
> *"At thirty-three, operating as a deep-lying playmaker in a tournament like the World Cup is a physical paradox, but Modric still anchors Croatia's engine room. His passing accuracy under high opposition pressure dropped by a mere 1.4% this season, which is exactly why their transition model remains perfectly functional despite an aging squad line-up."*

**Why this works:** Immediate statistical paradox, zero introduction delay, immediate deep tactical dive from sentence one, no generic filler text.

### Pattern 2 — Tactical Conflict Dialogue (Nesting quotes or tactical debates)
> *"— We cannot defend forty meters away from our goal against an attacker traveling at thirty-six kilometers per hour, — the manager noted during the press briefing. The solution was a deeper defensive low-block. It reduced their opponent's progressive passes by 22%, proving that tactical structure, not raw running speed, controls space."*

**Why this works:** Clean deployment of quotes, data integrated directly to substantiate tactical choices, avoids abstract emotional descriptions.

### Pattern 3 — The Statistical Contradiction (Advanced)
> *"Portugal's defensive block concedes an average of 0.74 xGA per 90 minutes in open play — a figure that ranks 4th best among all 48 World Cup squads. Yet their transition exposure rate climbs to 1.31 xGA per 90 when pressing lines are broken above the midfield line, which is precisely the structural gap that a side like Colombia's counter-attack is engineered to exploit."*

**Why this works:** Pairs a strong defensive metric against a conditional weakness, creating analytical tension without emotional language.

## 2. BANNED FOOTBALL CLICHÉS & TROPES
The following phrases signal amateurish, unscientific writing and must be instantly removed or rewritten into data:

1. *"The ball is round / unpredictable"* ──► Replace with: *"According to the Elo probability matrix, the match profile features a high standard deviation..."*
2. *"It's a game of two halves"* ──► Replace with: *"The team's PPDA intensity drops significantly after the 60th minute due to systemic fatigue..."*
3. *"They wanted the victory more"* ──► Replace with: *"Team A out-registered their opponents in progressive defensive actions and dual success rates by 14%."*
4. *"Goals win games"* ──► Replace with: *"An xG conversion differential of +0.45 ensured the clinical execution of their transition phase."*
5. *"Only time will tell"* ──► Replace with a definitive, conditional prediction: *"If Team B maintains a compact 5-3-2 low block, the likelihood of a draw scales to 42%."*
6. *"A tale of two halves"* ──► Replace with: *"The second-half PPDA value dropped from 8.4 to 14.7, indicating a systemic collapse in pressing intensity after the 60th minute."*
7. *"Heart of a champion"* ──► Replace with: *"The side registered 11 high-intensity sprints above 25 km/h in the final 15 minutes, a physical output metric consistent with top-tier closing performance."*

## 3. RHYTHM, PACING, AND SENTENCE VARIETY
- **The Conjunction Rule:** Exactly ~33% of sentences within any analytical piece must begin with a strong logical conjunction (*But, And, Yet, For, Thus, Consequently*) to mimic high-end technical sports journalism.
- **Vary Sentence Length:** Interlace punchy, data-heavy summary lines with expansive explanations of tactical mechanics.
- **No Emotional Bias:** Avoid words like "magical," "tragic," "miraculous," or "deserved." Substitute emotional assessments with statistical outputs (e.g., instead of "a miraculous save," use "a post-shot xG value of 0.88 successfully denied by the goalkeeper's positioning").
- **The Two-Sentence Rule:** No analytical claim may stand without either a supporting metric in the same sentence or a follow-up sentence containing the verifying statistic.

---

## 4. FORMATTING PROTOCOLS
- All variables, formulas, and math elements must use LaTeX notation or the dedicated `<span class="math">` tags.
- Percentages, distances, and speed velocity metrics must always be accompanied by the exact mathematical unit (%, m, km/h).
- All player names and national team names must use their **official FIFA English-language spelling** (e.g., Türkiye not Turkey, Côte d'Ivoire not Ivory Coast in formal contexts).
- Match scores and statistics must always be written in numerals, never in words (e.g., "7-1" not "seven to one").

---

## 5. TONE GUARDRAILS
- Never describe a national team using political, ethnic, or culturally loaded language. Refer only to tactical and statistical identity.
- Never assign moral superiority to any team or player. Performance differentials are expressed only in metrics.
- Do not editorialize refereeing decisions. If VAR or disciplinary data is relevant, cite the official FIFA match report.
